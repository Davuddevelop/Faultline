Campaign builder

Describe your robot. Get a config that runs.

Drop in your robot model and this reads it — joints, actuators, floor friction — then writes the campaign.yaml the harness expects. Everything happens in this browser tab.

Nothing is uploaded · no account · works offline once loaded

01 Your robot

A URDF or MJCF file — the physics description of your machine. Not CAD: a simulator needs joints, masses and inertias, which geometry alone does not carry.

Drop a model here, or choose a file .urdf · .xml · .mjcf
02 Your policy

The controller under test — the thing that reads sensors and drives the motors.

03 What to vary

The conditions to search through, in real physical units. Turn on what is plausible for your machine — a wider space needs a bigger budget to cover.

04 What counts as failure

Explicit rules you write, checked at every step of every run. Never a learned judgement — an assessor has to be able to check them by hand.

What this page does not do.

    
    
      
  • It does not run anything. The simulator is native code; your machine runs it.
    • 
      
  • It does not upload. Your model is read in this tab and never sent anywhere.
    • 
      
  • It does not convert CAD. STEP and SolidWorks files carry no joints or inertias.
    • 
      
  • It cannot check your model loads in MuJoCo — only that it parses as XML.
