What this report does not say.

  • No claim that the policy is safe. This campaign found violations; it cannot show their absence.
  • No claim of conformity with any regulation or standard.
  • Findings apply to the declared space above and to no condition outside it.
  • Minimal cases are locally minimal — no single axis relaxes further, but a different combination may be smaller.
  • The robot is a stand-in and the policy is a stance controller, not a trained one.
