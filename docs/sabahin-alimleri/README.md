## Order of work

1. **Supervisor first.** Without one, Forms 1, 1A and 3 cannot be signed. Send the message in `messages-az.md` today.
2. **Ask the organisers** the open questions (WhatsApp +994 51 816 78 17): the research plan template, and when the annotation and project form are due.
3. **Download the official forms** (links in the announcement) and copy the answers from `forms-draft.md` into them. Use the official files, not these drafts.
4. **You write the research plan** (the attachment to Form 1A) from `docs/` working plan. Sign everything **before** the experiment starts (rule 3.6).
5. Annotation and project form: **after** the results exist, in your own words.
6. Upload everything and **confirm** by 18 December. Don't wait for the last day.
