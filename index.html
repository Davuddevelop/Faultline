Why now

20 January 2027

Regulation (EU) 2023/1230, Annex I Part A

Self-learning safety functions stop being self-certifiable.

EU Machinery Regulation 2023/1230 replaces the Machinery Directive on that date — directly and identically in every member state. Machinery with fully or partially self-evolving behaviour falls under Annex I Part A, so a notified body must be involved even for a manufacturer following every harmonised standard.

And the delegated act defining what counts as adequate evidence isn’t due until 2028. Assessment becomes mandatory roughly eighteen months before anyone agrees what passing looks like.

Method

Five stages, one question.

  1. 01

    Ingest

    Your trained policy and robot model, loaded into deterministic simulation. Every run reproducible from its seed.

  2. 02

    Perturb

    Each test is a point in a parameter space: push force, friction, slope, sensor delay, actuator degradation, payload. Ranges are yours.

  3. 03

    Search

    Random sampling finds the obvious failures. A trained adversary finds the ones nobody thought to try.

  4. 04

    Detect

    Violations are explicit rules over the trajectory: tilt past a limit, contact force above a threshold, leaving a safe region.

  5. 05

    Report

    Failures clustered by mode, not listed by count. One document for engineers, one for the safety case.
